# Q37. Write short notes on Hailey–Hailey disease.

**Chapter**: Disorders of Keratinization | **Source Pages**: 192–193 | **References**: Rook's Textbook of Dermatology, Bolognia Dermatology, Andrews' Diseases of the Skin

---

## Answer

Also known as **familial benign chronic pemphigus**.

### Definition

It is an inherited **acantholytic disorder** characterized by erosions and blistering prominently in **flexures** and sites of friction and trauma.

---

### Epidemiology

- **Autosomal dominant** inheritance
- Onset typically in the **2nd–4th decade** of life

---

### Pathophysiology

- Disorder of **keratinocyte adhesion**
- **Missense/nonsense mutation** of the ***ATP2C1*** gene on chromosome **3q21-24**
- Reduced **SPCA1** (secretory pathway Ca²⁺/Mn²⁺-ATPase type 1) — a calcium pump in the **Golgi apparatus**
- Reduced trafficking of **desmoplakin** and **desmoglein-3 (Dsg-3)**
- Increased vulnerability to **ER stress**
- Altered **actin reorganization**

> **Key molecular distinction**: Darier disease → *ATP2A2* (SERCA2, **ER** calcium pump); Hailey–Hailey disease → *ATP2C1* (SPCA1, **Golgi** calcium pump). Both result in defective Ca²⁺-dependent desmosomal assembly, but through different organelle pathways.

---

### Triggers / Exacerbating Factors

- **Heat**
- **Friction**
- **Infection** (bacterial, fungal, viral)
- **UVB exposure**
- **Sweating**
- Contact allergy to adhesive dressings, ECG electrodes
- **Scabies**

---

### Clinical Features

- **Painful, pruritic, and often malodorous** lesions of flexures or other sites
- **Localized or generalized** → erythroderma
- **Folds** → fissuring, erosions, macerations, and vegetations
- Lesions may localize at sites of **inflammatory dermatoses** such as psoriasis, seborrheic dermatitis, or drug eruptions
- ***Nail***: **Linear white bands (longitudinal leukonychia)**
- **Oral**, esophagogastric, or vaginal involvement — rare

---

### Clinical Variants

1. **Papular acantholytic dermatosis of the genitocrural area**
2. **Relapsing linear acantholytic dermatosis**
3. **Mosaic variants**

---

### Severity Classification

| Grade | Description |
|---|---|
| **Grade 0** | Subclinical; carrier; asymptomatic / no lesions or nail changes only |
| **Grade 1** | Mild; localized to perineal or axillary; intermittent; responding to topical treatment |
| **Grade 2** | Moderate; chronic lesions at more than two body sites; locally severe and refractory to topicals |
| **Grade 3** | Severe; extensive or chronically disabling despite topical and systemic treatment |

---

### Complications

- Secondary **bacterial infections**
- **Candidal infections**
- **HSV** (Kaposi varicelliform eruption)
- **HPV**
- **Squamous cell carcinoma (SCC)**
- **Allergic contact dermatitis**

---

### Investigations

#### 1. HPE (Histopathology)

- **Suprabasilar clefting** with acantholytic cells lining and within clefts
- **Widespread partial acantholysis** at different levels of the epidermis, giving rise to the characteristic **"dilapidated brick wall"** appearance
- **Epidermal hyperplasia**
- **Corps ronds and grains** — rare (unlike Darier disease where they are prominent)
- **Neutrophils** — numerous, with vesicles or in parakeratotic crust

#### 2. Dermoscopy

- **Polymorphous vessels** (glomerular, linear, looped, spiral, and coiled) over a **pinkish-yellow background**

#### 3. Gene Sequencing

- Commercial **ATP2C1** gene sequencing available

---

### Treatment

#### First Line
- **Weight loss** (reduces friction in folds)
- **Emollients and topical antiseptics**
- Reduce **heat, friction, and sweating**

#### Second Line
- **TCS** (topical corticosteroids) alone or in combination
- **Oral antibiotics/antifungals/antivirals**
- **Oral prednisolone**

#### Third Line
- **Topical tacrolimus**
- Low-dose long-term antibiotics — **doxycycline**, **minocycline**
- **Oral cyclosporine**, **methotrexate**, **naltrexone**, **retinoids**, **vitamin D**, **magnesium chloride**
- **Flexural disease** — surgical ablation/excision, **breast reduction**, and **botulinum toxin (botox)**

---

### Key Differentiating Points: Hailey–Hailey vs Darier Disease

| Feature | Hailey–Hailey Disease | Darier Disease |
|---|---|---|
| **Gene** | *ATP2C1* (chr 3q21-24) | *ATP2A2* (chr 12q23-24) |
| **Protein** | SPCA1 (Golgi Ca²⁺ pump) | SERCA2 (ER Ca²⁺ pump) |
| **Distribution** | Flexural (intertriginous) | Seborrheic (central chest, face, scalp) |
| **Primary lesion** | Macerated erosions/vesicles | Greasy keratotic papules |
| **HPE hallmark** | "Dilapidated brick wall" — widespread partial acantholysis | Corps ronds and grains — full-thickness dyskeratosis |
| **Corps ronds/grains** | Rare | Prominent and diagnostic |
| **Nail changes** | Longitudinal leukonychia | Red and white longitudinal bands + V-shaped nicking |
| **Palmar pits** | Absent | Present |
| **Neuropsychiatric** | Not typical | Depression, epilepsy, suicidal ideation |

## Corrections & Verification Notes

- **"Corps rods"** (p. 193, HPE section) → **corps ronds** — The correct term is "corps ronds" (French for "round bodies"), describing round dyskeratotic cells with a pyknotic nucleus and perinuclear halo. "Corps rods" is a scan/typographical error.
- **"Sever:ty classification"** (p. 193, heading) → **Severity classification** — OCR/scan artifact splitting the word with a colon.
- **"drug eruption eruptions"** (p. 193, clinical features) → **drug eruptions** — Redundant duplication; should read "drug eruptions" (singular instance).
- **"Diclofenac gel 17"** (p. 192, Q36 treatment section; noted for continuity) → **Diclofenac gel 1% (or 3%)** — "17" is a scan artifact; diclofenac sodium gel is available as 1% or 3% formulations.
- **"w vesicles"** (p. 193, HPE section) → **with vesicles** — Truncated word in the scan.
- **Dsg-3 notation**: Source writes "Dsg-3" — verified correct; desmoglein-3 is the relevant desmosomal cadherin affected by impaired Golgi-dependent trafficking in HHD (Ref: Bolognia *Dermatology*, 4th ed., Ch. 56).
- **SPCA1 terminology**: Source correctly identifies SPCA1 as the Golgi calcium pump. Full name: **secretory pathway Ca²⁺/Mn²⁺-ATPase type 1** (Ref: Rook's, 9th ed.).
- **Chromosome 3q21-24**: Verified; *ATP2C1* maps to 3q21-q24 (Ref: OMIM #169600).
