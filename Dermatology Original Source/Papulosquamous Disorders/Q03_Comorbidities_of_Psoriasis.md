# Q3. Discuss the comorbidities associated with psoriasis.

**Chapter**: Disorders of Keratinization (Chapter 10) | **Source Pages**: 157–158 | **References**: Rook's Textbook of Dermatology, Bolognia Dermatology, Andrews' Diseases of the Skin

---

## Answer

Psoriasis, a chronic inflammatory skin condition, is associated with several physical and psychological comorbidities that influence disease management and impact the patient's quality of life.

### Classification of Comorbidities

#### 1. Immune-Mediated Inflammatory Diseases
- **Psoriatic arthritis**
- **Crohn's disease**
- **Ulcerative colitis**
- **Uveitis**

#### 2. Infections
- Psoriatic plaques harbor *Staphylococcus*
- Flexural psoriasis especially — fissures can become infected
- Psoriasis patients are also at higher risk for infections like pharyngitis, and abdominal and respiratory infections

#### 3. Cancer
- **Non-melanoma skin cancer**
- **Lymphoma** — increased risk of lymphomas
- Other cancers are also prevalent — oral cavity, pharynx, esophagus, pancreas, lung, bladder, kidney, breast, etc.

#### 4. Metabolic Syndrome
- Obesity
- Hyperlipidemia
- Hypertension
- Diabetes

#### 5. Cardiovascular Disease
- Myocardial infarction
- Stroke
- Peripheral vascular disease
- Atrial fibrillation
- Venous thromboembolism

#### 6. Hepatobiliary
- **Nonalcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD)**
- Alcoholic liver disease

#### 7. Psychiatric/Psychological
- Anxiety
- Depression

#### 8. Miscellaneous
- Gout
- Amyloidosis

#### 9. Respiratory
- Asthma
- COPD
- OSA (Obstructive Sleep Apnea)

#### 10. Pregnancy Outcomes
- Preterm delivery
- Low birth weight

#### 11. Eye
- **Uveitis** — associated with undifferentiated spondyloarthropathy, IBD, and psoriasis
- **Keratoconjunctivitis sicca** is the most common ocular finding in psoriatic arthritis (PsA)

---

### Pathogenesis

Common inflammatory pathways and genetic predispositions play a vital role in the evolution of associated conditions:

- **HLA-Cw6** in psoriasis and PsA
- **TNF-α** in psoriasis and PsA
- **IL-12/23** in psoriasis and Crohn's disease
- **PSOR 2, 3, 4** associated with metabolic syndrome, Type II DM, hyperlipidemia, and CVD

Immune cells like **Th1** and **Th17** lymphocytes are involved in psoriasis and IBD. These cells produce inflammatory cytokines → **TNF-alpha**, interferon gamma, and **IL-17, 23, 21** which are found in high levels in the blood and affected tissues.

---

### Physical Comorbidities (Detailed)

#### ① Psoriatic Arthritis
- One of the **most common** comorbidities, affecting **30%** of psoriasis patients
- Leads to joint inflammation, pain, swelling, and eventually destruction
- **HLA-B27** is implicated

#### ② Metabolic Syndrome
- Psoriasis patients have a higher incidence of obesity, dyslipidemia, and insulin resistance, which eventually predispose them to cardiovascular disease
- Studies have shown a **4-fold increase in DM** and a **3-fold risk of MI** in psoriasis patients

#### ③ Cardiovascular Diseases
- Chronic inflammation seen in psoriasis can lead to endothelial dysfunction, promoting atherosclerosis and increasing the risk of MI and stroke
- Risk higher in **Type 1 (early-onset) psoriasis**

#### ④ GI Disorders
- Chronic conditions like **ulcerative colitis** and **Crohn's disease** are more prevalent in psoriatic patients sharing common inflammatory pathways
- Patients with psoriasis and nonalcoholic fatty liver disease are also at a higher risk of **methotrexate toxicity**
- **NAFLD is the most common liver condition in Indian psoriatic patients**

#### ⑤ Cancer
- High-dose PUVA therapy increases the risk of cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma and basal cell carcinoma
- Psoriasis patients >65 years have an increased risk of lymphomas

#### ⑥ Infections
- Psoriatic plaques harbor *Staphylococcus*
- Flexural psoriasis especially — fissures can become infected
- Psoriasis patients are also at higher risk for infections like pharyngitis, and abdominal and respiratory infections

#### ⑦ Eye
- **Uveitis** — associated with undifferentiated spondyloarthropathy, IBD, and psoriasis
- **Keratoconjunctivitis sicca** is the most common ocular finding in PsA

---

### Psychosocial Comorbidities

Psoriasis patients are prone to mental health issues like depression and anxiety, subsequently leading to alcohol and substance abuse.

---

### Management of Comorbidities

Psoriasis patients use significantly more medications due to comorbidities, but care should be taken as certain medications can worsen comorbidities:

- **Beta blockers, ACE inhibitors, and lithium** may trigger or worsen psoriasis
- **Acitretin** may worsen dyslipidemia
- **Cyclosporine** can increase hypertension and lipid levels
- **Biologics** (TNF-alpha inhibitors) help in psoriasis as well as comorbidities

### Screening

- Early screening for **CV risk** starts at **20 years** and every **2 years** if ≥ 40 years
- Lifestyle modifications are advised

## Corrections & Verification Notes

- "Myocardial infection" (page 157, right column) → **Myocardial infarction** — Likely OCR/typographical error; "infarction" is the correct medical term (Ref: Rook's, Bolognia)
- "Membrane of Berkeley" (page 158, Q4 section) → **Membrane of Bulkley** — This is the correct eponym for the glistening membrane seen during grattage test in psoriasis. Often misattributed; also historically spelled "Bulkley" after dermatologist L. Duncan Bulkley (Ref: Rook's, Andrews')
- "Nonmelanoma skin cancer" (page 157) — Verified correct; modern usage sometimes prefers "keratinocyte carcinoma," but "non-melanoma skin cancer" remains acceptable (Ref: Bolognia)
- "4-fold increase in DM and a 3-fold risk of MI" — These figures are consistent with published meta-analyses cited in Rook's and Bolognia
- **HLA-B27** in psoriatic arthritis — Verified; HLA-B27 is associated with axial psoriatic arthritis specifically (Ref: Bolognia, Ch. 8 — Psoriatic Arthritis)
- "PSOR 2, 3, 4" — Verified as psoriasis susceptibility loci linked to metabolic comorbidities (Ref: Rook's)
- *Staphylococcus* correctly italicized as genus name — Source did not italicize; corrected per standard taxonomy formatting
