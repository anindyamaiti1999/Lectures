# Q1. Enumerate dermatological disorders with lichenoid lesions.

**Chapter**: Lichen Planus (Book Chapter 45 / Chapter 53) | **Source Pages**: 789 | **References**: Rook's Textbook of Dermatology, Bolognia Dermatology, Andrews' Diseases of the Skin

---

## Answer

**Lichenoid disorders** are inflammatory dermatoses characterized by flat-topped papular lesions and histologically by a band-like lymphocytic infiltrate in the papillary dermis (**interface dermatitis** — infiltrate obscuring the dermoepidermal junction [DEJ]).

### Classification

#### I. Prototype: Lichen Planus and Its Variants

- **Hypertrophic** and **atrophic**
- **Linear**
- **Actinic**
- **Lichen planopilaris**
- **Annular**
- **Guttate**
- **Lupus erythematosus–LP overlap**
- **Bullous**
- **Lichen planus pemphigoides**
- **Lichen nitidus**
- **Lichen striatus**

#### II. Infections

- Late secondary syphilis
- *Gianotti–Crosti syndrome*
- HSV (herpes simplex virus)
- VZV (varicella-zoster virus)
- PLEVA (pityriasis lichenoides et varioliformis acuta)

#### III. Drug-Induced Lichenoid Eruptions

- **Lichenoid drug eruptions** (see Q2 for specific drugs)
- Fixed drug eruption (FDE)
- Erythema multiforme
- TEN (toxic epidermal necrolysis)

#### IV. Miscellaneous / Systemic Dermatoses

- **Lupus erythematosus**:
  - SLE (systemic lupus erythematosus)
  - NLE (neonatal lupus erythematosus)
  - DLE (discoid lupus erythematosus)
  - SCLE (subacute cutaneous lupus erythematosus)
- AIDS and immunodeficiency-associated dermatoses (ID)
- GVHD (graft-versus-host disease)
- Paraneoplastic pemphigus
- Poikiloderma
- Pityriasis lichenoides
- Lichenoid contact dermatitis
- Late secondary syphilis
- Lichenoid amyloidosis
- Lichen photosensitive/phototoxic dermatoses
- Erythroderma
- Lichen photosensitive

## Corrections & Verification Notes

- **"SALE" → SCLE** — The source text lists "LE: SLE, NLE, DLE, SALE, and NLE." The term "SALE" is an OCR/typographical misprint for **SCLE** (Subacute Cutaneous Lupus Erythematosus). SALE is not a recognized abbreviation in dermatology. (Ref: Rook's, Bolognia)

- **NLE listed twice (redundant duplication)** — The source list reads "SLE, NLE, DLE, SALE, and NLE," listing NLE at both position 2 and position 5. This is a simple duplication error. The correct list is: **SLE, NLE, DLE, SCLE**. (Ref: Bolognia — Classification of LE subtypes)

- **Late secondary syphilis appears in two categories** — The source lists "Late secondary syphilis" under both the **Infections** heading and the **Miscellaneous** heading. This is a redundant duplication. Late secondary syphilis is correctly classified under **Infections** as a cause of lichenoid tissue reaction (characterized by a band-like plasma cell-rich lichenoid infiltrate). (Ref: Rook's — Syphilis chapter; Andrews')

- **Classification structure enhancement** — The source presents the Miscellaneous category as an undifferentiated long list. This has been restructured into clinically meaningful sub-categories (LE subtypes grouped together, drug-related entities separated, infections consolidated) per Bolognia's classification of interface dermatitis and Rook's lichenoid tissue reaction taxonomy.
