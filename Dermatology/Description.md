The Dermatology Folder contains explanations of MD Dermatology Q&A pairs of various chapters - for easy assimilation of the original material. That is why many subtopics & associated content are seemingly omitted. 

These are bad (have lots of important omissions) in terms of foundational literature - but conversely very useful for exam oriented study - both in terms of approach, coverage, and lucid explanations.

These explanations have been generated using AI (Claude Opus 5, Reasoning = High, via OpenRouter API) and more will be added gradually.
